How to try it

  1. Save the four photos above to Photos.
  2. In the app, open Closet, select My Photos, tap + and add the full-body photo.
  3. Select ALL, tap + and add the clothing photos.
  4. Tap the Fitting button in the middle of the tab bar. Choose Top & Bottom or One‑piece, put the items on the slots, then tap Start Fitting. The result takes about 10–30 seconds.
  5. Results are saved in StyleBook.
